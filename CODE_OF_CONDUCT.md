# Community Code of Conduct

We want MYTHOS X to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive project.

## Expected behavior
- Be kind, patient, and respectful.
- Offer actionable feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's personal information.
- Keep security discussions responsible and focused on authorized testing.
- Help newcomers learn.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, publishing private data, or encouraging unauthorized access and harm are not acceptable. Project maintainers may remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary.
